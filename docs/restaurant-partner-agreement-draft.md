## 1. Overview of Services

KosherEats operates a food ordering and delivery platform (the "Platform") that connects consumers ("Customers") with kosher-certified restaurants. KosherEats provides the following services to Restaurant:

- **Order Management**: A digital system for receiving, accepting, and managing customer orders via the KosherEats Seller App.
- **Payment Processing**: Collection of payments from Customers on Restaurant's behalf via integrated payment services.
- **Delivery Coordination**: Arrangement of delivery services through third-party courier providers (e.g., Uber Direct, DoorDash Drive) or KosherEats-affiliated couriers, at no cost to Restaurant.
- **Marketing and Visibility**: Listing of Restaurant's menu, branding, and location on the KosherEats consumer app and website.

---

## 2. Commission and Fees

### 2.1 Commission Rate
Restaurant agrees to pay KosherEats a commission of **seven percent (7%)** of the subtotal of each order placed through the Platform ("Commission"). The subtotal is defined as the total price of food and beverage items before taxes, delivery fees, tips, or any other charges.

### 2.2 No Additional Platform Fees
KosherEats does not charge Restaurant any signup fees, monthly fees, tablet fees, or marketing fees at this time. The Commission described in Section 2.1 is the sole fee charged to Restaurant.

### 2.3 Customer-Facing Charges
KosherEats independently sets and collects the following charges from Customers:
- **Delivery Fee**: A dynamic fee based on distance and courier availability, paid by the Customer.
- **Sales Tax**: Applicable sales tax as required by law, remitted to the appropriate tax authority.
- **Gratuity**: Optional tip from the Customer, directed to the delivery courier. No portion of tips is retained by KosherEats or deducted from Restaurant payouts.

### 2.4 Commission Adjustments
KosherEats reserves the right to adjust the Commission rate with **sixty (60) days' written notice** to Restaurant. Restaurant may terminate this Agreement without penalty within that notice period if it does not accept the new rate.

---

## 3. Payment Terms

### 3.1 Payout Schedule
KosherEats shall remit to Restaurant the order subtotal minus the Commission on a **weekly basis**. Payouts shall be initiated every Monday for the prior week's completed orders (Monday through Sunday).

### 3.2 Payment Method
Payouts will be made via direct bank transfer (ACH) to the account designated by Restaurant during onboarding. Restaurant is responsible for providing and maintaining accurate banking information.

### 3.3 Chargebacks and Refunds
If a Customer disputes a charge or requests a refund due to issues attributable to Restaurant (e.g., incorrect order, missing items, food quality), KosherEats may deduct the refunded amount from Restaurant's next payout. KosherEats will notify Restaurant of any such deductions.

If a refund is issued due to a delivery issue (e.g., late delivery, courier error), the cost is borne by KosherEats, not Restaurant.

### 3.4 Minimum Payout
Payouts below $25.00 may be carried over to the following week. No payout fees are charged to Restaurant.

---

## 4. Menu and Pricing

### 4.1 Menu Control
Restaurant retains full control over its menu items, descriptions, pricing, and availability on the Platform. Menu updates can be made at any time through the KosherEats Seller App.

### 4.2 Pricing Parity
Restaurant agrees that prices listed on the Platform shall not exceed prices offered through Restaurant's own direct ordering channels (in-store, phone, Restaurant's own website) by more than **10%**, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 4.3 Item Availability
Restaurant is responsible for keeping item availability current. If an item is unavailable, Restaurant should mark it as unavailable in the Seller App promptly to avoid order cancellations.

---

## 5. Order Acceptance and Fulfillment

### 5.1 Order Acceptance
Restaurant agrees to accept or reject incoming orders within **five (5) minutes** of receipt. Orders not acted upon within the acceptance window may be automatically canceled by the Platform and the Customer refunded.

### 5.2 Preparation Standards
Restaurant shall prepare accepted orders in a timely manner consistent with the estimated preparation time set in the Seller App. Restaurant shall notify the Platform when orders are ready for courier pickup.

### 5.3 Order Accuracy
Restaurant is responsible for ensuring that each order is complete, accurately prepared, and properly packaged for delivery (including appropriate containers, utensils, napkins, and any necessary temperature-maintaining packaging).

### 5.4 Cancellations by Restaurant
If Restaurant cancels an accepted order, the Customer will be refunded in full. Repeated cancellations (more than 5% of orders in a 30-day period) may result in temporary suspension or termination of this Agreement.

---

## 6. Kosher Certification and Compliance

### 6.1 Kosher Certification
Restaurant represents and warrants that it holds a valid kosher certification (hashgacha) from a recognized certifying authority and shall maintain such certification throughout the term of this Agreement.

### 6.2 Certification Changes
Restaurant shall notify KosherEats within **48 hours** of any change, suspension, or revocation of its kosher certification. KosherEats reserves the right to suspend Restaurant's listing immediately upon learning of a lapse in certification.

### 6.3 Kosher Standards Display
Restaurant's kosher certification authority and level (e.g., Cholov Yisroel, Pas Yisroel, Glatt, Bishul Yisroel) shall be displayed on its Platform listing. Restaurant is responsible for the accuracy of this information.

---

## 7. Licensing, Permits, and Food Safety

### 7.1 Regulatory Compliance
Restaurant shall maintain all licenses, permits, and certifications required by applicable federal, state, and local laws to operate a food service establishment, including but not limited to health department permits, food handler certifications, and business licenses.

### 7.2 Food Safety
Restaurant shall comply with all applicable food safety regulations and maintain standards consistent with local health department requirements. Restaurant is solely responsible for the safety and quality of all food prepared and provided through the Platform.

## 8. Intellectual Property and Marketing

### 8.1 License to KosherEats
Restaurant grants KosherEats a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Restaurant's name, logo, menu descriptions, and food photography for the purpose of listing and promoting Restaurant on the Platform and in KosherEats marketing materials.

### 8.2 Photography
KosherEats may, at its discretion and expense, arrange professional photography of Restaurant's menu items. Such photographs shall be owned by KosherEats but may be used by Restaurant for its own marketing purposes.

### 8.3 Reviews and Ratings
Customer reviews and ratings displayed on the Platform are the property of KosherEats. Restaurant may not incentivize, manipulate, or suppress Customer reviews.

---

## 9. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public business information of the other Party, including but not limited to financial terms, customer data, and operational details. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of **two (2) years**.

Restaurant shall not have access to individual Customer personal information beyond what is necessary to fulfill an order (e.g., delivery address, order contents). Customer contact information (phone, email) is masked or managed through the Platform.

---

## 10. Term and Termination

### 10.1 Initial Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of **one (1) year** ("Initial Term"), after which it shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless terminated as provided herein.

### 10.2 Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time with **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other Party.

### 10.3 Termination for Cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:
- Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice;
- Becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases operations;
- Loses a required license, permit, or kosher certification.

### 10.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- Restaurant's listing will be removed from the Platform;
- KosherEats shall remit any outstanding payouts to Restaurant within thirty (30) days;
- All confidentiality obligations shall survive as specified in Section 9.

## 11. Liability and Indemnification

### 11.1 Restaurant Liability
Restaurant shall be solely liable for any claims arising from the quality, safety, or preparation of food provided through the Platform, including but not limited to foodborne illness, allergic reactions, and incorrect allergen labeling.

### 11.2 Delivery Liability
KosherEats and its courier partners shall be liable for issues arising during the delivery process, including late delivery, food damage during transit, and courier misconduct.

### 11.3 Mutual Indemnification
Each Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from any third-party claims, damages, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from the indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement or negligent acts.

### 11.4 Limitation of Liability
In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceed the total Commission paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
