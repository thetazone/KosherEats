### [10/10] [android_courier] CourierRepository silently converts HTTP error responses into Result.success(emptyList)
**Files:** android/courier/app/src/main/java/com/koshereats/courier/data/repository/CourierRepository.kt, android/courier/app/src/main/java/com/koshereats/courier/ui/viewmodels/DashboardViewModel.kt
